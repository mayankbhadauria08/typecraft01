Lessons

Five levels, 78 lessons

Every lesson on today's site stays where learners left it. New ones fill the gaps: a review after every two new key pairs, real words as early as possible, and a boss checkpoint to close each unit.

on the site todaynew lessonnew boss checkpoint

Today one lesson introduces 17 symbols at once. The next version teaches them in three short lessons first and keeps the current lesson as the mixed review.

Inside every lesson: five steps

  1. new

    Watch

    A five-second 3D demo: which finger, and where it travels.

  2. today

    Keys

    The new keys alone, in short groups.

  3. today

    Words

    Real words built only from keys learned so far.

  4. today

    Sentences

    Full lines with spaces and rhythm.

  5. new

    Challenge

    A short timed round against the lesson's speed target.

Stars stay tied to accuracy, as today: one star at 80%, two at 92%, three at 98%. A crown is added for reaching the lesson's speed target at 95% accuracy or better.

Effects

Where the 3D and SVG work goes

3D

  • Keys with real travel, on a deck that tilts with the pointer machine above
  • Paper that feeds one line at a time, carriage return, bell machine above
  • Rolling mechanical counter for speed Speed reading
  • Game and lesson cards that tilt toward the pointer Arcade cards
  • Badges that flip over when earned 36 badges
  • Beat lane and race track drawn in perspective in the games

SVG

  • Ink that lands wet, then dries with a rough typewriter edge typed lines
  • Reach lines from the resting finger to the next key on the keys
  • Paper grain generated in the browser, no image files the sheet
  • Accuracy rings, key heatmap and practice calendar readings, progress
  • Medals and certificates drawn as vectors, sharp at any size rewards
  • A course route that draws itself as levels unlock in the build

One Effects setting covers all of it: Full, Light or Off. It also follows the device's reduce-motion setting, so the app stays quick on an old laptop.

How it gets built

Upgrade today's app and keep it fast

my pick

Upgrade the current app, no 3D engine

3D with CSS, effects with SVG and canvas, as on this page. Loads as fast as today, typing stays instant, accounts and saved progress carry over.

option

Rebuild on a 3D engine

More cinematic in screenshots. Slower first load, weaker on old laptops, and everything is rebuilt from zero.

option

New look only

Quickest to finish. No new lessons or games.

What I assumed. Change anything that is wrong.

Who it is for
Teenagers and adults learning to touch-type: students, job seekers, office staff. Friendly, not childish.
Language and keyboard
English on a standard QWERTY keyboard. No Hindi typing in this version.
Cost to learners
Free. Rewards are earned by practice, never bought.
Accounts
Works without signing in, as today. Signing in syncs progress across devices. Leaderboards wait for a later version.
Phones
Built for a real keyboard. A phone shows progress, badges and the course map.
Old progress
Every existing lesson keeps its identity, so nobody loses stars or scores.
